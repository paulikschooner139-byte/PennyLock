What PennyLock does

PennyLock helps you track daily expenses and stick to a monthly budget. When you overspend your goal, the app locks itself for a period you chose in advance — that is the self-commitment mechanism. No account is required and no data leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. PennyLock works entirely offline. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server.

Where is my data stored?

All transactions and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of its data permanently.

How does the lock work?

You set a monthly spending goal and a penalty lock duration. If you exceed the goal, the app locks for that duration. You can set the goal and the lock length at any time before overspending — that is the whole point of a commitment device.

I overspent and the app is locked. How do I get back in?

The lock ends automatically when the duration you configured runs out. On the Goal screen there is also a “Settle last month now” button, which closes out the previous month immediately so you can start a fresh cycle without waiting for the calendar to roll over.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone (long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete App). This removes every transaction and setting permanently. There is no backup on any server, because no server exists.

Does PennyLock take payments or subscriptions?

No. PennyLock is free. It contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.
