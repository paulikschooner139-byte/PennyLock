PennyLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

PennyLock is a personal budgeting and expense tracking app. Your privacy matters to us.

Data Collection

PennyLock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. All of your transactions, budget goals, and penalty settings are stored locally on your device only.

No Accounts, No Tracking

PennyLock does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not track your activity. There are no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs in this app.

Data Storage

All data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of your data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/paulikschooner139-byte/PennyLock.
